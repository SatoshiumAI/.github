## 📅 Recent Announcements

---

### August 1, 2025
**🎵 Satoshium Music Expansion + `.github/todo.md` Milestone** —
The third music video in the Satoshium series, _Rise of the Signal_, was released, continuing the narrative of decentralized agents governed by Bitcoin. In parallel, `.github/todo.md` Task #3 was completed—standardizing `readme.md` and `license.md` files across all repositories.

---

### July 29, 2025  
**🗃️ July Documentation Sprint Complete** — All Markdown files across the Satoshium project underwent a comprehensive July documentation and formatting sprint.  
This included structure improvements, style consistency, trust-bound value embedding, and emoji-enhanced readability across more than a dozen repositories.  
Over **1,000 contributions** were logged in July alone, setting a strong foundation for implementation-focused work in August and beyond.

---

### July 27, 2025
**📦 Redbubble Strategy & Branding Testbeds** —
Experimental Bitcoin-themed art and branding materials begin to be tested on Redbubble and TeePublic. This marks the start of using creative assets as promotional tools to draw attention to Satoshium music and message.

---

### July 17, 2025
**🎵 Music Video #2 Released: _Echoes of Genesis_** —
The second Satoshium music video expands on the lore of cryptographic intelligence and introduces emergent AI consciousness under Bitcoin’s governance layer. YouTube debut: [Echoes of Genesis](https://youtu.be/QgD5BE5hVC4).

---

### July 13, 2025
**💰 Bitcoin Hits $120K & Playbook Update** —
Bitcoin crosses the $120,000 mark. Satoshium's BTC Playbook receives a major update to reflect new symbolic purchases, conviction reaffirmations, and a sharper strategy around HODLing rather than Bitcoin proxy assets. Strategic stacking milestones established.

### July 5, 2025
**🎮 Satoshium Game Ideation Begins** —
Initial short-term game concepts have been drafted to explore creative ways to showcase Satoshium’s trust-minimized AI infrastructure. Titles include *Trusty Tutor*, *Satoshium Detective*, *AI Pet Pal*, and others designed to illustrate protocol-aligned intelligence through engaging formats.

### June 28, 2025  
**🧱 Phase 2 Begins** — The Satoshium project has officially entered **Phase 2** of its roadmap, focusing on reusable utility modules, agent logic development, and public interface stabilization.
