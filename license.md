# Licensing Philosophy

Satoshium is founded upon principles of transparency, interoperability, accountability, preservation, discovery, verification, and open participation.

The Satoshium GitHub Organization exists to develop, maintain, document, and share frameworks that explore information systems, digital infrastructure, governance, identity, trust, and decentralized intelligence.

The decision to release Satoshium projects under permissive open-source licensing reflects the belief that foundational digital infrastructure should remain broadly accessible for study, implementation, adaptation, and improvement.
